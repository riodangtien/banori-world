Animal artwork credits

Ready-made models used in BANORI World. Display scale, orientation and materials are adjusted to fit the forest. Original downloaded files and embedded textures are retained. Bear, boar and badger receive a reversible procedural lower-leg gait at runtime; squirrel uses a bounding motion. Wolf uses supplied skeletal animations.
