# BANORI World

BANORI brand identity landing page with a playable educational 3D forest demo.

https://riodangtien.github.io/banori-world/

## Editable world

The React/Three.js source lives in `world-src`. Run `npm ci`, `npm test` and `npm run build` there. The build writes directly to `forest` with relative URLs for GitHub Pages. Serve the repository root with any static HTTP server to preview the complete landing page at `/#world`.

Research, license decisions and preservation choices are documented in [research-notes.md](research-notes.md), [world-architecture-plan.md](world-architecture-plan.md) and [asset-credits.md](asset-credits.md). A Kenney canvas tent and two Quaternius animated animals ship under CC0; all other world geometry is generated by project source.

World opens with primary-school mathematics level selection. The demo reuses the existing forest, explorer controls, camera and collision surfaces. Reduced-motion preferences pause environmental animation initially; rendering stops when the parent switches away from World.

No change is published automatically. The static `forest` directory is ready for the repository's existing GitHub Pages publishing workflow after review.

The environment quality audit is in [WORLD-AUDIT.md](WORLD-AUDIT.md). Final automated checks and visual evidence are in [validation.md](validation.md), [placement-validation.json](placement-validation.json) and `qa/`. The source now includes bounds grounding, shared footprint validation, a rebuilt lookout and guardian camp, rooted ancient tree, variable-width water, animated wildlife, flowing/reflected/refracted surfaces, precipitation, fireflies and complete-island camera presets.

## Wildlife maths expedition

World is a fixed playable forest beneath the existing navigation. Start in Explorer view, move with WASD / arrow keys (Shift to run, Space to jump), and drag to look. V switches to Camera; F or CAPTURE photographs an animal. Touch joystick/look/jump controls remain available.

Find twelve different animal categories in any order: deer, fox, rabbit, frog, bird, butterfly, fish, wolf, bear, wild boar, badger and squirrel. Animals retain their existing habitats and behavior. A clear, centered photograph within 8 metres opens a Grade 1–5 maths question. Fish can be photographed through the transparent water surface. The question bank still covers numbers, shapes and measurement; the animal is the discovery that unlocks a challenge, rather than a physical maths station.

The former question tables/signs and their rendering components have been removed. Incorrect answers remain in the question with a hint, without teleporting animals or forcing a recapture. Correct answers award XP and add that species to the album; another individual of the same completed species cannot award more XP. Complete all twelve to finish the expedition. Pause displays the named species collection. Progress and safe player checkpoints persist on this device. Old station-demo saves use a separate key and are not resumed into this changed mechanic.

Explorer view uses the original CC0 human-explorer 3D model with Idle, Walk, Run and Jump animations. The official BANORI illustration is not rendered as the playable character until a faithful 3D model is available. Weather, motion settings, ambient audio and photograph download remain available. No new dependency was added.

Build: `npm --prefix world-src run build`. Serve the repository root and open `/#world`. Source tests cover the maths bank, all twelve animal capture categories, occlusion, duplicates, arbitrary discovery order, retries and persistence. Latest evidence is in `qa/wildlife/`; `qa/v2/` and its report describe the previous station-based iteration.

No push or deployment has been performed for this mechanic change.

## Ready-made wildlife models — 2026-10-15

The five procedural animal models were replaced after visual review. Wolf uses an animated Quaternius model (CC0); Black bear, Boar, Badger and Squirrel use complete models by Poly by Google (CC-BY 3.0), downloaded through Poly Pizza. Display height, orientation, grounding and rough materials are normalized to the existing deer style. Original geometry and embedded textures are retained. Wolf uses Idle, Walk and Eating animations. The other four downloaded models have no skeletal clips. They now roam with a reversible procedural lower-leg gait (bear, boar, badger) or a bounding motion (squirrel); the original downloaded GLBs remain unchanged.

The twelve-category collection, photography and maths flow remain. Previous seven-category progress is migrated. Artwork attribution is available from Pause → Animal artwork credits and in world-assets/animals/FOREST-MODEL-LICENSES.md. Sources and SHA256 records are in forest-model-sources.json. Model previews and gameplay checks are in qa/ready-models. No new dependency was added. No push/deployment was performed for this replacement.


## Expanded living habitats — 2026-10-15
The island radii are now 42 × 32 world units (formerly 28 × 22), giving 2.18 times the preceding land area. Outer pine hills, rocky ridges, flower meadow, old forest and wetland extend the existing ecosystem. Four connected waterways, eleven trail routes and seven automatically grounded bridges connect the interior and outer habitats. Deterministic placement yields 514 trees, 300 rocks, 36 logs and 48 stumps, with clear paths and reserved animal territories. Terrain, path, water and minimap bounds now follow the island dimensions rather than the old hardcoded rectangle.

Every habitat has a valid movement route. Larger mammals have wider territories; separate squirrel territories avoid borrowing rabbit clearance rules. The controller alternates roaming, idle and feeding, while keeping animals on dry, obstacle-free terrain. Existing downloaded artwork, the animated human explorer and the primary maths photograph mechanic remain in place. Validation: production build; 49 source tests, including ten-minute safe locomotion across all 20 habitats, per-habitat displacement and reversible mesh animation. Browser evidence is in qa/habitats. Changes are local, not pushed or deployed.


## Bridge and scenery refinement — 2026-10-15
Bridge rails now follow the same deck/approach profile as the player surface, with two continuous rails, regularly spaced capped posts, stone footings and transverse timber joists. Curved trail entrances connect to the nearest dry route with round-ended strips instead of detached rectangular patches. Existing movement/collision heights are preserved.

Ground cover uses rooted upright grass tufts instead of flattened pebble-like spheres. Sparse shoreline pebble groups avoid routes, bridge entrances and animal territories; mushrooms and reeds extend into the outer woodland and wetland. Reflection/refraction planes cover the enlarged island. Production build and the full 49-test regression passed, followed by all five navigation tests including the new dry-connector check. No push/deployment was requested.

## Contextual exploration HUD — 2026-10-15
The exploration view now keeps only compact controls at the edges: exit, one camera/explorer toggle, a small collection-progress pill, map and pause. The large quest card, permanent directions, XP display, minimap and persistent exploration prompts are removed from the play view. Failure feedback appears briefly; the album and climate settings are available in the pause journal, and the full field map opens on demand. The game scene remains visible while the player explores. Verified with a production build, 50 tests and browser checks for the map, journal and camera toggle.
